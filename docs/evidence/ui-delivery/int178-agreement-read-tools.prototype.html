What PAIGE says about agreements

INT-178 · the six answers the two read tools can produce · PR #1388

What to judge here: the wording, and whether each answer is the one you would want in that situation. Every string below is copied verbatim from the shipped adapter and the activity chip — this is not a mock-up of intended copy, it is the copy.

What this is not: a screen. Nothing about the chat surface changes — no route, layout, control or viewport. The only rendered element is the small grey activity chip, shown under each answer. Three of these six states exist because an empty result is the answer most likely to be stated as a confident falsehood, so each is told apart rather than flattened into “none”.

Deliberate: “viewed” means opened, not signed, and the tool description says so to the model. Signing evidence — IP address, device, the signature image — is never available here, so PAIGE cannot describe it even if asked.

2 · Genuinely nothing

the workspace really has none
You
Anything waiting on signatures?
Paige
Checked where your agreements stand · none matched
Nothing is out for signature right now.

This is the only state where “none” is the truthful answer — and the three below exist so it is never given in their place.

3 · An agency, which has no client book

top-level agency account
You
Where do my agreements stand?
Paige
Checked where your agreements stand · agency account — agreements live in the sub-account
This account is an agency, which manages sub-accounts rather than a client book, so it holds no agreements of its own. Agreements live in the sub-account that holds the client relationship — switch into it to see them.

Without this, the answer would have been “you have no agreements.” The database refuses agreement writes on a top-level agency, so the read truthfully returns nothing — and reporting that as an empty book would be a confident falsehood about an account that structurally cannot have one. Given only after an unfiltered read: a converted agency keeps its earlier agreements, so a filter matching none of them means “none matched”, not “this account holds none”.

4 · A client who is not in this workspace

stale or invented contact id
You
Has Priya signed yet?
Paige
Checked a client's agreement · that contact wasn't found in this workspace
No contact with that id exists in this workspace, so this is not “that client has no agreements” — the client could not be found at all. Let me look them up by name and try again.

Without this, the answer would have been “Priya has nothing outstanding” — a confident statement about a person the workspace may not have. The lookup runs as you, so a contact belonging to another workspace reads as absent, which is the correct answer to give you and discloses nothing about theirs.

5 · Reached the workspace, but not by membership

agency managing a sub-account · operator acting-as
You
Show me this sub-account's agreements.
Paige
Couldn't read your agreements
Agreements can only be read by someone with their own membership in this workspace. Access you hold by managing this account from a parent agency, or as a platform operator, does not extend to its agreements — so nothing can be shown here, and this is a limit rather than an error.

Without this, the answer was “this account is not a member of it” — which is not what happened, and sends you to fix an access problem you do not have. Three kinds of access reach a workspace without a direct membership row, and agreements do not travel down any of them today. Whether that limit should be lifted is a separate decision about the agreements read itself, not about this tool.

6 · The workspace changed underneath the question

switched accounts mid-turn
You
Where do my agreements stand?
Paige
Couldn't read your agreements
That workspace's agreements could not be read — the active workspace may have changed, or this account is not a member of it. Reopen the workspace and try again.

Deliberate: PAIGE never says which of the underlying refusals fired. The remedy is the same in all of them, and naming which gate refused would let someone probe for the difference.

Two refusals that never reach the database at all: a status filter that is not one of the eight real states, and a contact id that is not a well-formed id, are both refused before the read runs — because each would otherwise return an empty list that reads exactly like “you have nothing”.
