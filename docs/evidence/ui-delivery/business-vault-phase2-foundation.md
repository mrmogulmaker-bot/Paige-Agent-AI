## User job and state map

An owner or authorized administrator enters a tenant-bound Vault, sees only source-backed summaries, adds or edits owner-entered contract/obligation metadata, opens a focused inspector, and may archive records within current authority. Ordinary members fail closed before metadata. Binary actions show unavailable because no approved scanner exists. Workspace switches remount tenant-bound UI; focus/auth/periodic server revalidation clears navigation and content together after authorization loss. The Vault content region is the intended vertical scroll owner.
